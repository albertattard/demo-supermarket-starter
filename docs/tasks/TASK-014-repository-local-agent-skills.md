## Goal

Introduce workshop skills into the repository after reviewing their external source
and instructions. The attendee runbook describes the download and review exercise.

## Scope

- Download the published workshop skill archive and inspect its contents.
- Review each skill and its supporting files before using or adding it.
- Record the source, archive checksum, and review decision for the task-readiness skill.
- Commit accepted skills and the review record locally.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Accepted skill packages are available under `.agents/skills/` and committed locally.
- The review record identifies the downloaded archive and the reviewed files.
- The contributor makes the decision; checks and model assessments are evidence,
  not approval or proof of safety.
